Accurate Ruler

A ruler for iPhone and iPad that matches real inches and centimetres.

Most ruler apps assume every screen is the same size, so their inches are wrong on many phones. Accurate Ruler knows the exact screen of every iPhone and iPad model, and you can fine-tune it with a bank card or a real ruler.

What it does

  • Inch and centimetre scales from the same zero, with guide lines across the screen.
  • Fractions to 1/32″ or decimals, and millimetres to 0.1 mm.
  • Measure between two lines you drag. Your finger never covers the mark.
  • Measure things longer than the screen by sliding the phone along.
  • Bubble level, angle finder, unit converter and saved measurements.

What it doesn't do

No ads. No subscriptions. No accounts. No tracking. Nothing leaves your device.
