Privacy Policy

Accurate Ruler · Effective November 14, 2026

In short: Accurate Ruler does not collect, sell or share any information.

What stays on your device

Your settings, calibration and saved measurements are stored only on your iPhone or iPad. The app has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it doesn't connect to the internet.

Motion sensors

The level and angle tools read your device's motion sensors while they're open. The readings are used on the spot to draw the level and are not stored or sent anywhere.

Copying

If you copy a measurement, it goes to your clipboard, and from there only where you paste it.

Children

Accurate Ruler collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions are welcome on the Support page.
