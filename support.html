Common questions

Is it really accurate on my phone?
Accurate Ruler uses Apple's published screen specifications for your exact iPhone or iPad model. To double-check, open More › Calibrate and compare against a bank card or a real ruler.

Why measure between the lines instead of from the edge of the phone?
The phone's frame and rounded corners add a few millimetres. Lining an object up with the two measuring lines on the glass gives the true length.

How do I measure something longer than the screen?
Mark where the far line falls, tap Extend, slide the phone so the first line sits on your mark, and keep going. The total adds up as you go.

The bubble level seems off.
Put the phone on a surface you know is level and tap Set zero.

Where are my saved measurements kept?
Only on your device.
